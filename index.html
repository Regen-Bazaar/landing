The idea in four words

Impact, made into an asset

tRWI
Tokenized Real-World Impact. A token for a verified impact report, split into editions that many funders can own.
Impact Value
A score from a published formula (weights v0.1). Platform-assessed, not third-party certified.
EAS
Ethereum Attestation Service. The onchain record of each approved claim, checked before any token is minted.
USDG
A stablecoin issued by Paxos. Funders pay in it; the organisation receives it directly.
How it works

From a plain report to funded impact

Six steps. The AI only reads the report; a fixed formula does the scoring and a person approves.

01

Report

NGO / community

The organisation describes what it did in plain words: what, how many, where.

02

Extract

AI

AI pulls out the actions and numbers. It never sets the score.

03

Score

Published formula

A published formula computes the Impact Value, with a breakdown for every action.

04

Review

Human reviewer

A reviewer checks the report and approves it or sends it back.

05

Attest

Onchain

The claim is recorded onchain with EAS and gets its own generated tRWI artwork.

06

Fund

Funders

A funder buys editions in USDG. The organisation is paid in the same transaction.

Roadmap

Where it goes next

Ordered by priority, no dates. Everything today runs on test networks.

Phase 01
Public beta and feedback

Open testing with the community; fix every step where testers get stuck.

Now
Phase 02
Trust: methodology and verification

Weights calibrated with experts, required evidence, duplicate checks, a third-party verification tier.

Next
Phase 03
Onboarding without crypto

Sign in by email or Telegram, organisation profiles and teams, simple payouts.

Next
Phase 04
Funders, companies and AI agents

Card payments on a phone, a company portal with impact reports, an API for AI agents.

Later
Phase 05
Validator network and community

Independent validators with public track records, appeals, community rules.

Later
Phase 06
Real funds, after an audit

External security audit, multisig admin, legal review, launch with pilot partners first.
