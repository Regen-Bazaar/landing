01 · The problem

Impact funding is broken at the point of trust

Trillions are earmarked for impact, yet capital can't flow to the ground. Verification is slow, manual and opaque — so markets can't price impact, and good projects stay donation-dependent.

01
Verification is slow & expensive
Traditional MRV relies on manual audits and site visits — months of lead time and high fixed costs that exclude small projects entirely.
02
A crisis of credibility
Investigations into "phantom" carbon credits have eroded trust in offsets, leaving buyers wary and prices depressed.
03
No liquid market for impact
Impact outcomes aren't standardized assets — they can't be owned, traded or collateralized, so they attract no investors.
04
Donation dependency
NGOs and communities rely on grants and goodwill instead of durable, market-driven funding channels.
02 · The solution

A marketplace for tokenized Real-World Impact

Regen Bazaar packages measured impact into onchain Impact Products — verifiable, ownable and tradable. dMRV sits at the core, turning raw field data into trusted, priced assets.

03 · The moat

dMRV is the unlock

Digital Measurement, Reporting & Verification replaces manual audits with sensors, satellites, AI and onchain proofs — making impact cheap to verify, hard to fake, and instantly tradable.

Multi-source measurement

IoT sensors, satellite & remote sensing, and field evidence captured at the source.

AI-assisted verification

Models cross-check claims against data, flag anomalies and compress months of audit into minutes.

Onchain provenance

Every datapoint and verification is anchored onchain — an immutable, double-counting-resistant trail.

Standards-aligned

Built on the same Measure–Report–Verify logic used by leading standards like Verra and Gold Standard.
