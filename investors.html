01 · The problem

Impact funding is broken at the point of trust

Trillions are earmarked for impact, yet capital can't flow to the ground. Verification is slow, manual and opaque, so markets can't price impact, and good projects stay donation-dependent.

01
Verification is slow & expensive
Traditional MRV relies on manual audits and site visits: months of lead time and high fixed costs that exclude small projects entirely.
02
A crisis of credibility
Investigations into "phantom" carbon credits have eroded trust in offsets, leaving buyers wary and prices depressed.
03
No liquid market for impact
Impact outcomes aren't standardized assets. They can't be owned, traded or collateralized, so they attract no investors.
04
Donation dependency
NGOs and communities rely on grants and goodwill instead of durable, market-driven funding channels.
02 · The solution

A marketplace for tokenized Real-World Impact

Regen Bazaar turns a plain impact report into an onchain tRWI: verifiable, ownable and fundable. A published scoring formula and human review sit at the core.

01

Report

NGO / community

The organisation describes its work in plain words; AI extracts the actions and numbers.

02

Score

Published formula

A fixed, versioned formula computes the Impact Value. The AI never scores.

03

Review & attest

Reviewer · EAS

A person approves; the claim is recorded onchain and listed as a tRWI.

04

Fund

Funders

Funders buy editions in USDG; the organisation is paid in the same transaction.

03 · The moat

Scoring anyone can audit

The Impact Value engine is the part built from scratch. It makes small projects cheap to assess and every score explainable. Weights are v0.1: platform-assessed, not third-party certified.

AI reads, it does not score

An LLM extracts actions and quantities from free text. The report is treated strictly as data and the output is validated.

Deterministic, versioned formula

34 action types across six domains, each mapped to SDGs. Every score carries its table version and a per-factor breakdown.

Human review, onchain record

A reviewer approves each report. The sale contract checks the EAS attestation before any token is minted.

Next: calibration

Weights calibrated with domain experts against established frameworks, and selected actions in physical units such as tCO₂e.

06 · Roadmap

From public beta to real funds

Ordered by priority, no dates. Phases 2 and 3 are what we would take on first with funding.

Public beta and feedback

Open testing with the community; fix every step where testers get stuck.

Now
Phase 02

Weights calibrated with experts, required evidence, duplicate checks, a third-party verification tier.
