01 · Why

Why list your impact as a tRWI?

A tRWI turns a report into something a funder can buy, hold and check, and opens funding beyond grant cycles.

01

Paid directly

Each sale pays your wallet in the same transaction, in a stablecoin.

02

No certification cost

Nothing to pay up front. The platform takes a small fee from each sale.

03

A record donors can check

Every approved report gets an onchain record anyone can look up.

04

Beyond grant cycles

Funders can support work you have already done, not only proposals.

05

Any kind of impact

Environment, animal welfare, education, poverty, social and health.

06

Open scoring

You see how your Impact Value is calculated, line by line.

02 · Before you submit

What to prepare

The stronger your evidence, the faster a reviewer can approve your report. Here's what to have ready.

✓
Who you are

Organization type (NGO, community, social enterprise) and whether you're officially registered.

✓
Actions taken

What you did: cleanups, workshops, plantings, programs.

✓
Impact achieved

Numbers, for example 1,000 trees planted, 10 workshops, 1,500 kg of waste collected.

✓
Period & location

When it happened and the area of impact (country, province).

✓
Evidence

Photos, reports, data or third-party records that support your claims.

✓
A payout wallet

A regular wallet address (for example MetaMask) that receives the funds.

03 · The process

From report to funding

AI only reads your report. A published formula does the scoring and a person approves.

Step 01
Describe

Write what you did in plain words. AI picks out the actions and numbers.

Step 02
Score & review

The formula computes your Impact Value (weights v0.1, platform-assessed, not third-party certified). A reviewer checks it.

Step 03
Record & list

The claim is recorded onchain and listed as a tRWI with its own generated artwork.

Step 04
Get funded

Funders buy editions; 97.5% of each sale goes to your wallet in the same transaction.
