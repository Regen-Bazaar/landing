02 · Before you apply

What to prepare

The stronger your evidence, the faster we can verify and tokenize. Here's what to have ready.

✓
Who you are

Organization type (NGO, community, social enterprise) and whether you're officially registered.

✓
Actions taken

What you did — cleanups, workshops, plantings, programs.

✓
Impact achieved

Measurable outcomes — e.g. 1,000 trees planted, 10 workshops, tons of waste collected.

✓
Period & location

When it happened and the area of impact (country, province).

✓
Evidence

Photos, reports, data or third-party records that support your claims.

✓
Web presence

A website or social media so we can verify your project.
